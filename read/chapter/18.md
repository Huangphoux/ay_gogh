The other day, Mrs. Smith was at her friend Daisy’s birthday party.

(When it is your birthday, you have a party for your friends.)

Some days before, the postman had brought her a letter from her friend.

In the letter Daisy wrote that she would be very glad to see her at her birthday party on the next Wednesday, and that she wanted her to come to dinner at seven o’clock.

“Look here,” Mrs. Smith said to her husband and gave him the letter, “an invitation to my best friend Daisy’s birthday party, and it is not an invitation to afternoon tea, but to dinner, and later we are to have chocolate.

How glad I shall be to put on my new frock for the first time!

You will dine in town then that day, my dear, will you not?”
